# Essentialism v3: storyboard draft (for approval, nothing rendered)

## What changes from v2

| Area | v2 | v3 draft |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 75 s | About 2 min 30 s (about 380 words) |
| Depth | Each takeaway in one sentence | Each takeaway = the finding + **how to use it this week**, with an example for a busy manager |
| Illustrations | 1 whiteboard scene, 7 text cards | 7 hand-drawn whiteboard scenes (icons, a decision flowchart, a comparison), alternating with dark cards for rhythm |
| Design | Numbers wrap ("10+ min"), lots of small text | Short stats only, one idea per screen, headline of 6 words or fewer, a closing "this week" checklist |
| Structure | Summary, then 4 tips | Problem, 2 big ideas, 4 deep takeaways, verdict, recap checklist |

Claim IDs ([C-xx] book claim, [S-xx] research that replaces a weak claim) point to `01-research/claims-table.md`.
Lines marked *(practical tip)* are our advice built on the evidence, not a research finding.
