## Why these choices

- **Access level C:** no file was provided, so this is the honest level. It also tests the engine's "secondhand" labeling.
- **2014 edition:** the core text most reviews and studies refer to. The 2024 additions are noted, not analyzed.
- **8-minute anchor plus 90-second cut:** the Output Catalog asks for 8 to 15 minutes, while the existing
  Explainer16x9 composition is built for 45 to 90 seconds. Writing the full script and rendering a short cut
  tests both the framework and the video handoff without touching the video pipeline.
- **No CTA and no lenses:** this is an informative Track A piece; lenses are optional and would add a thesis.
