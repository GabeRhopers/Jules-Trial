The Song of Zink

Wake in the shrine, find the hookshot and the bombs, and silence the Warden of the Bell.



  

    

      
Controls

      
    

    

      
← →
Move

      
Z / Space
Jump, hold for higher. Against a wall: wall jump

      
X
Needle. Hold ↑ to aim up, ↓ in the air to pogo

      
C
Dash (one in the air)

      
A
Hookshot (once found)

      
S
Bomb (once found)

      
D hold
Song heal: 3 notes for 1 heart

      
↑
Rest at a bench (heals and saves)

    

    
Needs a keyboard. Progress saves in this browser at benches and when you pick up items.
