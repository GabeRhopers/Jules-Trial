The Song of Zink

Wake in the shrine, find the hookshot and the bombs, and silence the Warden of the Bell.

Turn your phone sideways to play

Xbox layout shown. On PlayStation use the same positions: ✕ jump, □ needle, ○ dash, △ hookshot, R1 bomb, L1 heal. Press any controller button once to connect it.

    
On a phone, turn it sideways: the pad moves Zink (push up to rest or aim, down in the air to pogo) and the buttons on the right act. Hook and Bomb buttons appear once you find those items. Progress saves in this browser at benches and when you pick up items.
