# Stop paying twice for rejected anodized parts

- **Brand:** demo-b2b-industrial
- **Format:** Explainer16x9 (target 45 to 90 s)
- **Audience:** Procurement and quality managers buying anodized aluminum parts
- **Goal:** lead (drawing submitted for spec review and quote)
- **Structure:** hook, problem, solution, 3 benefits with proof, CTA

> All figures (98% first-pass yield, 10 days, 96% on time, 24 h) are demo placeholders. Replace them with
> the client's real, documented numbers before publishing.

## Narration (source of truth: `config.json`)

**hook:** Every rejected lot of anodized parts costs you twice: once in rework, and again in missed ship dates.

**problem:** Most rejects trace back to three specs nobody wrote on the drawing: coating class, seal test, and color tolerance.

**solution:** So we review every drawing before production, and lock those three specs with you in writing.

**benefit-1:** The result: first pass yield above 98 percent, across every lot we shipped last year.

**benefit-2:** Lead times you can plan around: 10 working days, with 96 percent of orders on time.

**benefit-3:** And a test report with every lot, so incoming inspection takes minutes, not hours.

**cta:** Send us your next drawing. You get a spec review and a quote within 24 hours.
