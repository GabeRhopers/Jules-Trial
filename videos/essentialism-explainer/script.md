## Narration (source of truth: `config.json`)

**hook:** Busy all day, and nothing that matters moved? One best-selling book says you're simply doing too much.

**idea:** In Essentialism, Greg McKeown argues that only a few things are truly vital. So choose them on purpose, and say no to the rest.

**method:** His method has three steps. Explore what really matters. Eliminate everything else. Then execute, until the essential becomes routine.

**scorecard:** We checked fourteen of the book's claims against the research. Six hold up. Three only partly. Two are contested, and three can't be tested at all.

**holds-up:** The practical tools hold up best. In one study, students expected to finish a thesis in about 34 days. They took 55. So yes, build in a buffer.

**shaky:** The weaker parts: its biggest ideas come from the author's own experience, and some of the science it cites has since been revised, or failed to replicate.

**verdict:** Our verdict: worth reading if other people's requests run your calendar. Trust the tools, and treat the big claims as opinion, not science.
