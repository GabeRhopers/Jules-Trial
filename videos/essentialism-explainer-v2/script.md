## Narration (source of truth: `config.json`)

**hook:** Busy all day, and nothing that matters moved? The book Essentialism says: do fewer things, on purpose. Here's what actually works.

**mindset:** Big idea one is a mindset. You always have a choice. Only a few things are truly vital. And every yes is a trade-off.

**method:** Big idea two is a method. Explore what really matters. Eliminate the rest, with a graceful no. Then execute, until the essential becomes routine.

**holds-up:** Its practical tools hold up. In one study, students expected to finish a thesis in about 34 days. They took 55. So build in a buffer.

**swap-plans:** But we found no study testing its 90 Percent Rule. Research shows if-then plans work: if a new request comes in, then ask what it replaces.

**swap-breaks:** The book says play sharpens your thinking, but adult evidence is thin. What works: short breaks restore energy. After hard work, you may need more than ten minutes.

**swap-tracking:** And small wins as the top motivator is a stretch. What works: track your progress, in writing. It raises your odds of reaching the goal.

**verdict:** The verdict: read it for the tools, especially if other people's requests run your calendar.
